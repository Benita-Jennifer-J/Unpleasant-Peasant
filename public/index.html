The Unpleasant Pleasant

Help for peasants. Not good help. But help.

Welcome, wretch! Thou hast found Thatch Support, where our learned Brothers solve thy woes. None of them can read, but all are very confident.

Plague? Taxes? Cow in the bed? Bring it hither. Our cures are Bad, Worse, and sometimes Flammable.

WarningBishops, witches, and tax men should not enter. Also people with good sense. They never stay long anyway.

Printed in 1665 by a man with a cough.

    Tell Us Thy Woe

    Pick one from the list, or say it in thine own words. Our Brothers shall ponder it for as long as it taketh to find the door.

    Woes Peculiar to Thy Parish

    Woes Commonly Heard

    Many have suffered before thee. Open a notice to read what we told them. None came back to complain.

    Posted Upon the Door

    Notices of the parish, nailed up in no order.

    Lost

    One cow. Also the field she was in. Reward: the field.

    Wanted

    Honest man to hold a horse. Dishonest men may hold two.

    Closed

    The pump is shut until the pump can be found.

    Sermon

    Sunday. The subject is thee. Attendance is not optional, and neither is the subject.

    Questions Nobody Asked

    Art thou qualified?
    We own a hat. We share it.
    Wilt thou answer me soon?
    Sooner than thou canst fix it, which is not hard.
    Is thy advice safe?
    It is advice. Safety was never in the charter.
    May I speak to a manager?
    Our manager is a goose. She biteth.

    Thatch Support is not liable for plague, fire, flood, taxes, witches, or the Dutch.

    Open from cockcrow till the candle dieth. Closed on holy days, unholy days, and Tuesdays.

    For urgent matters, shout at the nearest hill and wait for an echo.
